Evil Word Find

You are looking for the word "kayak". Tap letters to select them. When you find it, you win!

Settings

The Evil Word Find settings are in the Settings app on iOS.

Evilness

"Fair" will ensure one of your words appears at least once. "Neutral" just leaves it to chance so there may or may not be a word to find. Evil Word Find won't block against extra occurrences, but is nice enough to match any one that you find.

Dimensions

These are kind of a mystery... yeah. Just don't make the board bigger than you can read, because then you won't be able to read it and it'd probably get slow but you might not know since you can't read it.

Words

You can put your own words in. Give it just one or give it a few. Palindromes are fun. Words are separated by commas and can contain spaces and punctuation. What's the hardest word set you can come up with?
