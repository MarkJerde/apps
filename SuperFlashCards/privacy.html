Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Nothing that happens in Super Flash Cards leaves Super Flash Cards, unless you share a deck or enable ads. Advertising partners may access your advertising identifier on iOS 13 or lower and will be provided information about any flash card decks which are not specified as "sensitive" in the deck settings simply to provide ads that are more relevant to you. This deck setting is shown at the time you create a deck and any time it is edited thereafter, with a fair bit of explanation of this option shown along with it. Your Super Flash Cards settings are managed by iOS, so iOS has access to them and will do what it does. Any of this could change in the future, but not now.
